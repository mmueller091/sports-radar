How the rating works.

Live scores and game details come from public ESPN feeds and refresh each minute. If the feed cannot be reached, the status line labels the fallback as demo data.

Before playMatchup stakes, rankings, and rivalries. A scheduled 0–0 is never treated as a close game.
During playCurrent score margin and how far along the game is.
After playFinal margin, overtime, lead changes, and decisive scoring plays when available.
Betting linesShown when ESPN lists them. MLB and NHL may offer a moneyline rather than a run/puck spread. Post-start pregame lines are not labeled as verified closes unless the feed says so.
Ranking modelEach card explains its rating in plain language. Favorites are pinned first; all other games are sorted by rating. The 1–99 score is an estimate, not a league statistic.
