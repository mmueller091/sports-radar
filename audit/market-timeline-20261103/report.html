Market root cause and correction

The deployed site does not load a separate pregame-outlook.js. Its active implementation is pregameExplanation in app.js. It inferred market closeness from broad scoring-factor labels (“Near pick’em” / “Competitive betting market”). The scoring helper scans opening and closing prices and considers the smallest absolute price; that signal is unsuitable for describing the current favorite. A separate NHL/MLB branch only overrode it at -200 or shorter. Thus both -166 and -162 fell through to “neither side much of an edge.” The captured current displayed prices and paired quote data themselves were present and usable.

The new deterministic editorial helper reads the same current game.line that is displayed. It accepts a paired quote only when displayed price, provider, team identities where supplied, and current quote representations agree. It never mixes opening and closing prices. It removes bookmaker margin by dividing each implied probability by the paired sum. Sums outside 1–1.12 are rejected. One-sided prices receive a conservative margin allowance and can never independently produce an “even” or “heavy” classification. Conflicting quotes produce a neutral inconsistency statement. Upcoming displays no longer fall back to an older cached line when current evidence disappears.

Deployment and remaining limits

The release uses the existing Pages workflow and a clean focused commit. Deployment is permitted only after all gates pass and origin/main is rechecked. The final delivery includes the resulting commit, workflow and actual-production verification receipt. No force push or history rewrite is used. Rollback is a normal revert or redeployment of the preserved baseline.

Market labels are conservative editorial conventions, not calibrated forecasts. Missing/currently inconsistent markets remain unclassified; one-sided prices cannot fully remove margin. Record percentages do not establish schedule strength or player availability. Partial historic observations cannot be repaired retroactively from the available evidence, so their gaps remain visible. Final quality stays retrospective even when its number happens to match the last live rating.
