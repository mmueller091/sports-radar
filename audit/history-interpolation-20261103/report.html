What caused the disconnected dots

The previous renderer incremented its segment after every incomplete or invalid observation, required identical segments and strictly increasing game-clock position, and used straight line commands. Its existing monotone curve helper was unused. Identical same-clock repeats were collapsed visually, while changed ratings at the same clock created breaks. Pelicans–Grizzlies therefore had 97 disconnected sections (72 isolated dots) and only 39 visible connections, despite 296 verified live observations.

Connection policy

The new display layer references existing verified anchors. It never creates intermediate snapshot objects or modifies the canonical observation mapping. Every interval checks real timestamps, game/team identity when supplied, exact model-version equality, nondecreasing cumulative team scores, sport-specific phases, supplied intermediate evidence, and integrity flags. Incomplete observations may be bridged; contradictions, unresolved corrections, non-live transitions and incompatible models remain breaks. Sparse coverage stays disconnected beyond five minutes without an observed break or three minutes of unseen game time. A zero clock alone cannot establish an end. Period transitions need a verified end record or a second verified forward observation in the new phase.

Monotone cubic geometry passes through the anchors exactly. Ordered, bounded control points prevent overshoot and additional turning points; a segment falls back to a straight line if its controls would reverse. Small dots identify real ratings; lighter lines are estimates and dashed portions bridge partial observations. Point interactions show recorded ratings, times and actual scores. Segment interactions name both anchors and do not report an inferred rating or game event. Opening and retrospective final quality remain separate from the live curve.

SportChronological rule
NFL / NBA / NCAAMCanonical quarters or halves and numbered overtime, each with its established duration. Countdown must not increase. NFL regular season and postseason formats remain distinct.
CFBFour timed quarters; overtime is untimed and plotted at real observation timestamps, with ordered overtime numbers.
NHLThree periods; regular/postseason overtime duration preserved; shootout follows overtime and cannot return to play. Unknown extended-overtime format fails closed.
MLBActual top/bottom half-inning order, including extras, at observed timestamps. Missing half-inning evidence does not establish a connection.
TennisVerified consecutive sets must agree with cumulative sets won. The current history contract lacks ordered game/point evidence, so within-set connections remain disconnected; no synthetic clock or tennis event is added.
