## Current tension and history

Regulation live CFB current tension uses the existing margin closeness multiplied by `1 - .18 * smoothstep(clamp((gap - 3) / 11))`. A field-goal-or-less margin is unchanged; the discount grows continuously through one- and two-possession margins, reaching 18% at 14 points. Comeback runway, feasibility, generic leverage, and overtime tension retain their existing calculations. This is a general margin adjustment, without an event-ID or halftime-score override.

Recent CFB run credit is proportional to scoring that reduces a deficit or first takes a one-possession lead. Charlotte's first seven points in the 14-point run turn a four-point deficit into a three-point lead; the next seven extend that lead. Credit becomes two points. Pure comeback runs retain four; pure lead extensions receive zero. Lead-change and upset calculations are preserved.

The removed run allowance is also removed from the shared history cap, scaled by existing live-history relevance. Otherwise a previously capped probability swing or positive trajectory would refill the same space and erase the correction. At this checkpoint the cap becomes 13, leaving seven lead-change, two run, and four WP points. Tests exercise attempted trajectory refill up to 100 points.
