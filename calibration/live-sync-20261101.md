# Verified live evidence and viewing priority

Model: `2026-11-01.live-evidence.1`. Baseline: deployed commit `c6f123046322706ff0d23d9d60225b1dc9d668f7`.

## Root causes and changes

The production runtime rebuilt history and trajectory from `null` whenever a summary was missing or rejected. This erased verified past drama. The legacy adapter's short carryover was based on an aggregate score difference, required unchanged scores, and was overwritten by the production pass. The new production path retains bounded historical evidence and recalculates it with current margin, relevance, clock and feasibility. It does not smooth or limit real score changes. The scoreboard continues to own score, clock and possession; a rejected summary no longer prevents a valid scoreboard advance. Chronology checks now distinguish overtime periods beyond the regulation progress clamp.

Historical evidence and current observations have separate inputs. Retained scoring events and the last verified trajectory describe the past; current win probability is null during a summary gap. Event recency, margin relevance and history caps are recalculated. No scoring event is synthesized from a scoreboard increment, and newly accepted evidence replaces the cache rather than adding another copy of its factors. Empty detail payloads do not erase known history. Score decreases, invalid scoring ledgers, incompatible identities, excessive clock movement and expiry invalidate carryover. An unreconciled overturned-touchdown ledger supplies no historical/event-shock credit. Recorded rating observations, including the original 21–27 observation, remain unchanged.

Football preparation unconditionally restored the observed baseline's underdog, including null. It now recovers a separate underdog identity from explicitly closing/pregame fields when that observed baseline lacks a market. Closing spreads must have compatible sides; markets explicitly marked live are rejected. The observed opening rating, factors and baseline object remain unchanged. The recovered market survives refreshes and is passed once through the existing upset/context formula.

NHL on-ice and goalie-roster omissions could remove a verified power play without a state change. Confirmed manpower now has independent retention: fresh explicit lineups override it, missing goalie classification can use previously verified goalie identities, and absent manpower can briefly retain a confirmed advantage. Known penalty timing, goals, period changes, fresh strength evidence and expiry end retention promptly. Ordinary history retention never turns cached win probability into a current estimate.

Halftime changes only viewing priority: live NFL/CFB/NBA/NCAAB/Soccer games receive a six-point sorting adjustment during a confirmed halftime. The displayed rating stays unchanged; resumption immediately removes the adjustment. Favorite pinning and the intentional All/Final membership/count rules are unchanged. Cards label the temporary lower viewing priority. NCAA end-of-regulation is not mistaken for halftime.

Live descriptions use sport-specific phases: football Q4/OT, NBA's final six minutes/OT, hockey's final ten minutes/OT, college basketball's final five minutes/OT, baseball's seventh inning onward, and soccer from 75 minutes. Description changes do not alter numeric scoring.

Both CFB examples recover five upset points at the captured states. Separately, verified closing odds can inform the existing market-and-score fallback when no current WP is available; this does not modify the observed opening baseline. Genuine loss of current WP/pressure can still change a rating; the no-closing-market CFB, EKU and entirely-missing NHL cases illustrate this. Their historical evidence is retained without pretending that current WP or pressure is still known.

The original observed Oklahoma State sequence **53 → 75 → 90 → 64 → 91** and Toronto sequence **69 → 82 → 69 → 82** are retained in the compressed fixtures. The repaired historical prefix reconstructs the old accepted CFB rating as 86 rather than the originally observed 91: not every contemporaneous input/revision was archived. The additional five points in the new comparison are independently explained by recovered underdog credit. Historical NHL on-ice lineups were unavailable; the manpower replay is a disclosed controlled 5-on-4 variation using the real teams, score, clock and two-minute tripping record. It is not presented as a captured historical lineup.

## Expiry, cost and validation

Historical carryover is bounded to two minutes of observation time and two minutes of game-clock movement. Normal adjacent regulation periods can preserve past facts; regulation/OT changes require fresh evidence. Manpower is confined to the same period and score, its known penalty expiry, and at most two minutes of observation time. Without a positioned penalty, its clock runway is limited to 15 seconds. Cached historical evidence is capped at 24 KB, 100 scoring records and 1,024 historical probability values; oversized ledgers are not silently truncated.

Ephemeral evidence lives in the existing current-feed state. It is excluded from the long-term rating snapshot payload, verified by a D1 ingestion regression. No new tables, migrations, providers, polling intervals, AI calls, paid APIs or recurring services are introduced. Existing data acquisitions, schedules, retention and notification behavior remain in place.

Eight new regressions cover accepted/missing/stale/empty summaries, TD/PAT advances, overturned scores, evidence expiry, period/OT changes, current-WP isolation, both real closing markets, repeated scoring passes, live-market rejection, NHL manpower changes, halftime sorting/resumption, wording boundaries, All/Final membership and ingestion storage. The complete suite passed **780 tests with zero failures/skips**; the final halftime guard was also rechecked with the relevant UI and scoring tests. Both legacy score/timeline fixture groups pass. Desktop and mobile browser checks verify card order, unchanged halftime ratings, Game Center, timelines, descriptions, no JS errors and no horizontal overflow. Browser-bundled and Worker scoring agree on accepted and retained-evidence probes.
